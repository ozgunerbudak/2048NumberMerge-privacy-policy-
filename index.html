Privacy Policy for 2048 Number Merge

Effective date: September 13, 2026

Lumorca Software built 2048 Number Merge as an offline game. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information collection and use

2048 Number Merge does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app does not require an account and does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party data collection services.

Data stored on your device

The app stores game progress, scores, statistics, and preferences locally on your device so that your game and settings can be restored. This information is not sent to Lumorca Software or to third parties. You can remove it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Permissions

The app may use your device's vibration feature to provide optional haptic feedback. It does not use this permission to collect information.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Security

Because the app does not transmit personal information, no personal information is stored on our servers. Locally stored app data remains subject to the security of your device and operating system.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. Any revision will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the privacy policy repository.
